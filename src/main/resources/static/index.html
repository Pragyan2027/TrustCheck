TrustCheck

Company verification and suspicious offer-letter screening

TrustCheck provides risk indicators, not a definitive legal judgment. A real company can have a suspicious offer, and a scammer can copy a real company's details. Verify important findings independently.

Verify a company

Check a company against your blacklist dataset and available public signals such as office listings, domain history, website security and DNS records.

Providing a website enables more checks. A missing website alone does not prove a company is fake.
Free email providers or a domain mismatch are caution signals, not proof of fraud.

Check an offer letter

Upload a text-based PDF or TXT file, upload an image for on-device text recognition, or paste the letter text. The analyzer looks for payment demands, suspicious wording, email/domain clues, salary claims and company identifiers.

Scanned/image-only PDFs may not contain extractable text. Upload a screenshot/photo instead. Image OCR runs in your browser; PDF text is sent to your Spring Boot server for analysis.

Built with Java and Spring Boot · Results are automated indicators, not proof of fraud.
